Single-material shell jacket

An outer layer made from one material family, so it can be repaired and recycled as one.

Brief

Starts from T1: the fabric itself as the form. One material carries shell, trims and closures, and the construction is shown, not hidden. It would be right for Vollebak's materials-first products, or for Marfa Stance's modular outerwear.
