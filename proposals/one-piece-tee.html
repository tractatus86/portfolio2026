One-piece tee with a material record

A tee cut with almost no waste, carrying a record of what it is made of.

Brief

Starts from T3: rematerialization, and an intention that can be checked on the object. The tee is cut from one piece of cloth, and a small label or digital record states fiber, mill and making. It would be perfect for A-POC ABLE Issey Miyake, built on one piece of cloth.
