Resoleable trail sneaker

A trail sneaker built to be resoled, so its wear becomes part of its look.

Brief

Starts from T9: wear and repair as the source of value. The upper is made to age visibly and the sole is a replaceable part, so the shoe gets better with use instead of being thrown out. It would suit Flower Mountain, whose trail line already carries a Japanese design story (T8).
