Work pant that records wear

A work pant whose fabric and dye are chosen to fade where the wearer moves.

Brief

Starts from T9: wear as a real trace of time, not an applied finish. The fabric and dye are chosen so the knees, seat and pockets change with use, and the pant ships clean. It would be perfect for Buck Mason's lived-in American classics.
