Coastal sun hat

A sun hat whose colors and weave come from one stretch of coast.

Brief

Starts from T6: an object placed in a landscape, and what it leaves there. Colors are read from one place, and the materials are chosen for what happens to them outdoors. It could sit in a La Jolla Group surf or outdoor brand, or in Catch Surf's loud, playful line.
