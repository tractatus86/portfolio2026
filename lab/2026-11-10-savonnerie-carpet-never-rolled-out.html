Savonnerie: the carpet that never got rolled out (lineage)

"92 carpets, one corridor": Louis XIV's Savonnerie commission as the largest woven project of its time.

Theme: T1 Fabric as sculpture, sculpture as cloth · Topic: Textile structure · Status: idea

Proposal

Theme reason: a monumental woven carpet scheme read as large-scale textile work.

One-line idea: "92 carpets, one corridor": Louis XIV's Savonnerie commission as the largest woven project of its time.

Why it matters: Lineage: commissioned 1668, woven over about 20 years after designs by Charles Le Brun, never laid as planned. Link: scale, pile depth and modular repeat are the same questions as modular carpet tile and large-format soft flooring today.

Format and visual direction: Carousel of 4. (1) Your own line diagram: 92 panels across a 440 m span, shown as a long strip; (2) a close pile cross-section sketch (knot, pile height, ground weave); (3) a repeat-and-seam study of how adjacent panels meet; (4) a short caption on labor and scale. Palette: deep red, gold ochre, ivory, graphite lines.

Alt text: Line drawing of a very long strip divided into numbered carpet panels beside a cross-section of a knotted pile.

Opening line: "The largest carpet ever woven was 92 pieces, and the king never used it."

Idea: The Met's "A King's Carpet: Louis XIV and the Savonnerie" is on view from 8 Sep 2026 to 5 Mar 2028 (lineage item, not new this week). Per the Met, the scheme was meant to cover the Louvre's Grande Galerie, 440 m by their figure; the show has four complete carpets, fragments, a trial weave, and an in-gallery conservation studio. The museum also describes a young, expendable workforce of trainee weavers. Interpretation: the interesting design question is modularity, since a single image had to survive 92 hand-woven seams. Note on the Met's own figures: one page says 440 m and another says 480 yards, so quote 440 m only with attribution, and to confirm.

Source line: https://www.metmuseum.org/press-releases/kings-carpet and https://metmuseum.org/exhibitions/a-kings-carpet-louis-xiv-and-the-savonnerie
