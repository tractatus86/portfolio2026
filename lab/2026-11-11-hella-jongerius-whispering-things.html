Hella Jongerius: Whispering Things (product design / colour and material lineage)

"Design that whispers." Three decades of color, textile and craft-versus-industry experiments, shown through the studio archive of tests and prototypes.

T4 Institutional critique and the apparatus · Trend colours

Why it matters

More than 400 objects (furniture, textiles, ceramics, prototypes, sketches, films) on show at MK&G Hamburg, organized by the Vitra Design Museum; the museum states the show covers her work on color, materials and fabrication, with projects for Camper, IKEA, Vitra and MoMA (MK&G exhibition page, accessed 11 Nov 2026). Curator: Glenn Adamson (Vitra Design Museum page). Her archive of color and material tests shows that CMF is a research practice, which is the story a portfolio should tell.

The idea

The failed swatch is part of the design.

Jongerius's show turns the test shelf into the exhibit: prototypes and experiments next to the finished products. Do the same with your own library: three swatches, what each one got wrong, and what you changed. It shows range and process, which a finished render cannot.
