Hella Jongerius: Whispering Things (product design / colour and material lineage)

"Design that whispers." Three decades of color, textile and craft-versus-industry experiments, shown through the studio archive of tests and prototypes.

Theme: T4 Institutional critique and the apparatus · Topic: Trend colours · Status: idea

Proposal

Theme reason: the studio archive of tests shown as the exhibit, exposing the apparatus behind design.

One-line idea: "Design that whispers." Three decades of color, textile and craft-versus-industry experiments, shown through the studio archive of tests and prototypes.

Why it matters: Fact: more than 400 objects (furniture, textiles, ceramics, prototypes, sketches, films) on show at MK&G Hamburg, organized by the Vitra Design Museum; the museum states the show covers her work on color, materials and fabrication, with projects for Camper, IKEA, Vitra and MoMA (MK&G exhibition page, accessed 11 Nov 2026). Curator: Glenn Adamson (Vitra Design Museum page). To confirm: run dates differ between sources (MK&G page: 23 Oct 2026 to 13 Jun 2027; Vitra page and IKEA Stiftung: 16 Oct 2026 to 30 May 2027); designboom also says 23 Oct to 13 Jun. Interpretation: her archive of color and material tests shows that CMF is a research practice, which is the story a portfolio should tell.

Format and visual direction: Carousel, 3 to 5 slides. Your own photographs of a small set of material and color tests (swatches, yarn wraps, a failed attempt kept on purpose), arranged on a neutral ground with handwritten test labels. One slide with a single sentence on what each test taught you.

Alt text: A tidy grid of woven and dyed fabric swatches in muted colors, each labeled by hand as a test.

Opening line: The failed swatch is part of the design.

Idea: Jongerius's show turns the test shelf into the exhibit: prototypes and experiments next to the finished products. Do the same with your own library: three swatches, what each one got wrong, and what you changed. It shows range and process, which a finished render cannot.

Source line: https://www.mkg-hamburg.de/en/exhibitions/hella-jongerius-whispering-things and https://www.design-museum.de/en/exhibitions/detailpages/hella-jongerius-whispering-things.html and https://www.designboom.com/art/exhibition-radar-november-2026/
