Diedrick Brackens: weaving as story

"Lights at the end of the world": handwoven tapestry that joins West African weaving, American quilting and European tapestry.

T1 Fabric as sculpture, sculpture as cloth · Textile structure

Why it matters

Lineage: three weaving traditions in one practice. Link: warp/weft decisions (color change, edge, density) carry meaning, a useful frame for interior fabrics that must also tell a story.

The idea

"Every color change in a tapestry is a decision."

Baltimore Museum of Art lists the exhibition from 8 Nov 2026 to 28 Feb 2027, with recurring images of light, domestic interiors and horses, and new poetry by Joël Díaz. domestic interior as subject makes this relevant to interior designers, not only fine art audiences. Keep the post about technique and reading cloth, and avoid describing individual works not seen.
