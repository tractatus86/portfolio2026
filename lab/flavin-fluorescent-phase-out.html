Flavin and the fluorescent phase-out

A design input matched with a market input. First dry run of the post workflow: four colorway mocks for a carousel.

The pair

  • Design input: Dan Flavin, "Some Light" opens at Guggenheim Bilbao on 11 Nov 2026. His medium is the fluorescent tube. Images in the design note.
