Fried, not fired: Cecil Nabenashi, "Tempura Object" (product / craft)

"What if frying were a forming method like firing or casting?"

Theme: T9 Wear, fragility and the trace of time · Topic: Material innovation · Status: idea

Proposal

Theme reason: soft paper and fiber is fixed into a fragile object, and the marks of the process stay visible.

One-line idea: "What if frying were a forming method like firing or casting?"

Why it matters: Fact: at Designart Tokyo 2026 (30 Oct to 8 Nov), Tokyo Midtown Galleria, Cecil Nabenashi shows vases and bowls made from paper and fibers coated in batter and deep-fried; the exhibition text compares it to firing ceramics or casting, a way of solidifying material between chance and control (designweek.app listing for Designart Tokyo 2026). Dezeen (5 Nov 2026) reports she used a batter of specially developed non-edible ingredients and that her next challenge is a deep-fried garment. She was selected for the festival's UNDER 30 program (PR Times release, Designart Tokyo). Lineage: craft as a set of techniques for controlling material (the show's own framing). Interpretation: for a soft-materials designer this is a finishing study: a coating plus heat changes color, hardness and surface of a fiber structure, which is close to how stiffeners, resins and heat-set finishes work on textiles. Durability and safety are untested here (to confirm).

Format and visual direction: Single image or 3-slide carousel. (1) The top view of bowls and tangles on white. (2) A swatch of your own fiber or yarn before and after a coating test. (3) One line on what changed: color, hand, stiffness. Palette: cream, pale gold, white. Owner could make: coat a yarn bundle in a flour or starch paste, dry or bake it, shoot it on white.

Alt text: Pale golden bowls and loose tangles made of deep-fried paper and fiber lie on a white surface.

Opening line: She fried paper to make a bowl, and the oil did the sculpting.

Idea: Frying here is a hardening step, like a finish, not a recipe. I would ask the textile version: which of our coatings and heat treatments change a yarn's shape as much as its surface? Pair the bowls with your own before-and-after yarn test.

Source line: https://www.designart.jp/designarttokyo2026/exhibitions/13817 (Designart Tokyo 2026 listing); https://www.dezeen.com/2026/11/05/designart-tokyo-2026-highlights/ (Dezeen, 5 Nov 2026); https://builtcurrent.com/articles/designart-tokyo-turns-10-with-recyclable-tape-and-deep-fried-vases (Nov 2026, date to confirm)
