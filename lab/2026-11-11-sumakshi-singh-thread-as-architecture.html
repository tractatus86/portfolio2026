Thread as architecture: Sumakshi Singh, "Permanent Address" (fine art / fiber art)

"A house made of thread." A demolished family home rebuilt life-size in translucent white thread, shown next to handwoven cotton by Ranjani Shettar.

Theme: T9 Wear, fragility and the trace of time · Topic: Textile structure · Status: idea

Proposal

Theme reason: a demolished family home rebuilt in thread as a trace of what is lost.

One-line idea: "A house made of thread." A demolished family home rebuilt life-size in translucent white thread, shown next to handwoven cotton by Ranjani Shettar.

Why it matters: Fact: at the India Pavilion, 61st Venice Biennale, Singh rebuilds fragments of her demolished New Delhi family home in white thread, drawing on embroidery traditions passed through generations of women; Shettar's "Under the same sky" combines handwoven cotton, steel and lacquer (designboom, 16 Jun 2026). Lineage: embroidery and handwork as an archive of domestic space. Interpretation: for an interiors and soft-materials designer, this is cloth standing in for walls: sheerness, density and light are the structure.

Format and visual direction: Carousel, 4 slides. (1) Singh's thread house reference pairing, (2) a close-up of your own sheer-structure study, (3) a line drawing of the same structure as a plan, (4) one line on density and light. Palette: white, warm gray, one pale shadow tone; matte-on-sheer contrast.

Alt text: White thread outlines of a room's walls and doorway hang in space, drawn as a delicate sheer structure.

Opening line: What if a wall were only thread, and the light did the rest?

Idea: Singh remakes a lost house from thread alone, so what you see is the idea of the building, not the building. As someone who works with density and openness in cloth, I read it as a construction study: how few yarns can still hold a room? Pair it with a sheer of your own and compare how each one lets light through.

Source line: https://www.designboom.com/art/india-pavilion-venice-biennale-2026-craft-idea-home-alive-interview/ (designboom, 16 Jun 2026; show runs 9 May to 22 Nov 2026)
