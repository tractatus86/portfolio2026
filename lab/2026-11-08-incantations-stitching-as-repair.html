Incantations: stitching as a ritual of repair (fiber art)

"Quilt as spell" - Kristina Snook, Incantations, Textile Art Gallery NYC.

Theme: T9 Wear, fragility and the trace of time · Topic: Textile structure · Status: idea

Proposal

Theme reason: patching and seams as repair and the trace of handwork.

One-line idea: "Quilt as spell" - Kristina Snook, Incantations, Textile Art Gallery NYC.

Why it matters: Exhibition runs 3 to 28 Nov 2026, opening 7 Nov, artist talk 21 Nov (per Fiber Art Now listing). The artist describes working with fabric as personal ritual, arranging elements until they hold the weight of the moment. Lineage (labeled): quilting as a domestic, repair-based craft. Link to textiles: pieced construction, seam as structure, patching as pattern.

Format and visual direction: Carousel of 3. Own photos of a pieced or patched textile: full view, seam macro, back side showing stitch paths. Keep palette warm and worn; show thread tension and uneven edges.

Alt text: Close view of a patchwork textile with visible hand stitching crossing several fabric edges.

Opening line: Every seam is a decision to keep something.

Idea: Incantations treats stitching as an act that turns intention into object. As a designer, I read the seam map as structure: where pieces meet, where thread carries load. The pairing shows the back of a piece beside the front, because the construction is the story.

Source line: https://www.fiberartnow.net/state/new-york (Fiber Art Now listing, Nov 2026; gallery page to confirm)
