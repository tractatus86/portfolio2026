Incantations: stitching as a ritual of repair (fiber art)

"Quilt as spell" - Kristina Snook, Incantations, Textile Art Gallery NYC.

T9 Wear, fragility and the trace of time · Textile structure

Why it matters

Exhibition runs 3 to 28 Nov 2026, opening 7 Nov, artist talk 21 Nov (per Fiber Art Now listing). The artist describes working with fabric as personal ritual, arranging elements until they hold the weight of the moment. Lineage (labeled): quilting as a domestic, repair-based craft. Link to textiles: pieced construction, seam as structure, patching as pattern.

The idea

Every seam is a decision to keep something.

Incantations treats stitching as an act that turns intention into object. As a designer, I read the seam map as structure: where pieces meet, where thread carries load. The pairing shows the back of a piece beside the front, because the construction is the story.
