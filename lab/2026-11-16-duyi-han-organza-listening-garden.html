Duyi Han, "it's raining outside, it's raining inside": pleated organza and embroidered lyrics

A Shanghai villa turned into a "Listening Garden" of pleated organza sculptures, a rain-ripple carpet and embroidered song lyrics.

T1 Fabric as sculpture, sculpture as cloth · Textile structure

Why it matters

Designer and artist Duyi Han installs the solo show at VILLA tbh, Shanghai, 24 Oct to 22 Nov 2026. A custom carpet printed with concentric rain ripples anchors pleated translucent organza sculptures; lyrics and film dialogue are embroidered into the fabrics; backlit textiles soften the bathroom (designboom, 28 Oct 2026; submitted by the artist through designboom's reader-submission feature). Lineage: sheer drapery and embroidered text as domestic ornament. An interiors and soft-materials designer can read it as a study in how sheer layers, pleat depth and embroidery density change a room's light and sound; the acoustic effect is not stated by the page and stays a hypothesis to test with Engineering or Lab.

The idea

What does a room sound like when the curtains are the sculpture?

Han puts three textile jobs in one room: a printed carpet, sculptural organza and embroidered lyrics. As a designer of surfaces and structure, I see one question: how much embroidery can a sheer carry before it stops being sheer? Pair the install view with your own organza and embroidery test and compare thread density against light.
