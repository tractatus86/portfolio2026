Duyi Han, "it's raining outside, it's raining inside": pleated organza and embroidered lyrics

A Shanghai villa turned into a "Listening Garden" of pleated organza sculptures, a rain-ripple carpet and embroidered song lyrics.

Theme: T1 Fabric as sculpture, sculpture as cloth · Topic: Textile structure · Status: idea

Proposal

Theme reason: translucent organza is built into sculpture and soft furnishing at once, so cloth is both object and room.

One-line idea: A Shanghai villa turned into a "Listening Garden" of pleated organza sculptures, a rain-ripple carpet and embroidered song lyrics.

Why it matters: Fact: designer and artist Duyi Han installs the solo show at VILLA tbh, Shanghai, 24 Oct to 22 Nov 2026. A custom carpet printed with concentric rain ripples anchors pleated translucent organza sculptures; lyrics and film dialogue are embroidered into the fabrics; backlit textiles soften the bathroom (designboom, 28 Oct 2026; submitted by the artist through designboom's reader-submission feature). Lineage: sheer drapery and embroidered text as domestic ornament. Interpretation: an interiors and soft-materials designer can read it as a study in how sheer layers, pleat depth and embroidery density change a room's light and sound; the acoustic effect is not stated by the page and stays a hypothesis to test with Engineering or Lab.

Format and visual direction: Carousel, 4 slides. (1) Wide view: carpet plus organza figures. (2) Close-up of an embroidered organza edge. (3) A backlit sheer panel. (4) One line on pleat depth and light. Palette from the photos: apricot, rose, brown, cream; sheer against dense pile. You could shoot or render your own organza-with-embroidery swatch lit from behind and set it beside slide 3.

Alt text: A pleated pink organza figure, a hanging fringed lamp and embroidered sheer shoes stand on a patterned rain-ripple carpet in a wood-floored room.

Opening line: What does a room sound like when the curtains are the sculpture?

Idea: Han puts three textile jobs in one room: a printed carpet, sculptural organza and embroidered lyrics. As a designer of surfaces and structure, I see one question: how much embroidery can a sheer carry before it stops being sheer? Pair the install view with your own organza and embroidery test and compare thread density against light.

Source line: https://www.designboom.com/art/duyi-han-rain-inspired-textile-listening-garden-shanghai-villa/ (designboom, 28 Oct 2026; older than 48 h, widened because it fits the organza and embroidery interest)

Hashtags: #organza #embroidery #textileinstallation
