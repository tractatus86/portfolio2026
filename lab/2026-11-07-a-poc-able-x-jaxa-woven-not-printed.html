A-POC ABLE x JAXA: woven, not printed (product / textile structure)

"Antarctica in the weave" – jeans where a satellite image is built into jacquard density, not printed on.

T3 Dematerialization vs rematerialization · Textile structure

Why it matters

Lineage: Issey Miyake introduced A-POC ("A Piece of Cloth") in Paris in 1998 (per Herald Business). The exhibition says the cloth structure could extend to interiors and architectural components. Link to textiles: image-as-structure is the same logic as automotive jacquard seat fabrics, argued from art rather than specs.

The idea

The image isn't printed on the denim; the weave itself draws it.

"The Possibilities of a Piece of Cloth, TYPE-XVII" runs at 21_21 Design Sight, Tokyo, 21 Oct to 19 Nov 2026. A-POC ABLE varied weave density to render ice and sea tones, with a heat-shrink "Steam Stretch" fabric prototype developed with the Tomohiro Tachi lab (University of Tokyo). No price or sale date is confirmed by the company, so I am not mentioning release.
