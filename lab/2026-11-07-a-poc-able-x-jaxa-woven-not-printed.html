A-POC ABLE x JAXA: woven, not printed (product / textile structure)

"Antarctica in the weave" – jeans where a satellite image is built into jacquard density, not printed on.

Theme: T3 Dematerialization vs rematerialization · Topic: Textile structure · Status: idea

Proposal

Theme reason: an image built into the cloth structure instead of printed on it.

One-line idea: "Antarctica in the weave" – jeans where a satellite image is built into jacquard density, not printed on.

Why it matters: Lineage: Issey Miyake introduced A-POC ("A Piece of Cloth") in Paris in 1998 (per Herald Business). The exhibition says the cloth structure could extend to interiors and architectural components. Link to textiles: image-as-structure is the same logic as automotive jacquard seat fabrics, argued from art rather than specs.

Format and visual direction: Single image or 3-slide carousel. Left: ice/sea image in grayscale. Right: your own jacquard float-density map of the same crop (white = long float, black = tight binding). Color: indigo, ice white, gray; matte cotton-denim hand.

Alt text: Side by side, a gray satellite image of cracked ice and a woven-density map of the same crop.

Opening line: The image isn't printed on the denim; the weave itself draws it.

Idea: "The Possibilities of a Piece of Cloth, TYPE-XVII" runs at 21_21 Design Sight, Tokyo, 21 Oct to 19 Nov 2026. A-POC ABLE varied weave density to render ice and sea tones, with a heat-shrink "Steam Stretch" fabric prototype developed with the Tomohiro Tachi lab (University of Tokyo). No price or sale date is confirmed by the company, so I am not mentioning release (to confirm).

Source line: https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10924971 (4 Nov 2026) and https://floortok.com/journal/issey-miyake-jaxa-apoc-able-21-21-design-sight-2026 (1 Nov 2026)
