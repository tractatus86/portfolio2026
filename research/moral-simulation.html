Method · multi-model debate

Moral Simulation

A method for having several AI models, each given a different role, argue a question with a built-in tension, to produce an insight no single answer gives. Started from Walter Benjamin on AI-flooded creative content.

Goal
have several AI models, given different roles, produce an insight beyond a single answer (from the playbook: "Running a Debate Experiment"). The method is reusable; Run 000 is the only run so far.
Why its own project
it began inside Theoretical Research (2026-11-05). Concepts and the log of artists, works and ideas stayed in Theoretical Research.

Concepts the experiment started from

All three come from Walter Benjamin; they live in Theoretical Research and are cited here. | Concept | What it is | How Run 000 used it | |---|---|---| | Aura | Unique presence of an original artwork; weakened by mechanical reproduction ("The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction") | Models disagreed on whether AI content kills aura or only exposes how exclusivity is manufactured | | Flâneur | An idle city walker who watches and collects impressions (the Arcades) | Asked whether scrolling a feed is flâneur-like; sharpest line: the feed does the walking, so the user is audience, product and data at once |

The readings behind them are in the Benjamin Reading List.

The method

Roles
Defender, Framework Skeptic, Materialist, Practitioner, Historian of the Concept, Reverse Devil's Advocate.
Playbook
one question with a tension; 3 or 4 roles plus a separate moderator; a fresh chat per model; the organizer only carries text and does not steer; one question per round, same full transcript to every model.
Evaluation Checks
question answered, reasoned changes, real disagreement, novelty, framework challenged, honesty, specificity. A run failing Novelty and Real disagreement is a summary, not an insight.
