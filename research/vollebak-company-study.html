Vollebak company study

Market-entity study of Vollebak Ltd (London): ownership, funding, filed assets, positioning, products and materials. Revenue and market share not found.

Scope: market entity only (revenue, assets, share, positioning, products and materials). No mood board (Agent 2). Label key: FACT = in a cited source; ESTIMATE = third-party modeled figure; INTERPRETATION = my reading.

1. Summary

FACT: Vollebak is a small, venture-backed London direct-to-consumer brand selling premium men's technical apparel built around named high-performance materials (graphene, Dyneema, carbon fiber). Revenue is not public. Filed assets are small (about £5.1M total, about £0.7M net). INTERPRETATION: It sells material stories and objects more than volume. Evidence of scale is thin, so treat it as a material-innovation reference, not a market-share player.

9. Materials worth a closer look (for soft materials, apparel, car interiors)

Hypotheses, not recommendations to buy or test: 1. Dyneema (UHMWPE) woven as a grid into nylon: abrasion and rip resistance at low weight; relevant to seat bolsters and door trim wear zones. 2. Graphene coated membrane or yarn: brand claims thermoregulation and bacteriostatic action; needs independent data before any car-interior use. 3. Carbon fiber and Dyneema in a ripstop with elastane: stretch plus strength; relevant to stretch-compatible trim. 4. Glass-sphere resin coating (Black Squid): retroreflective, color-shifting surface; a surface-effect option, durability and hand unknown. 5. Recycled-PET insulation and Re:Down: sustainability angle for quilted trim. Limits: all from brand pages. No supplier-level or test data. Agent 5 (Lab) or Agent 3 (Supply Chain) would need to confirm before use; both awaiting information.
