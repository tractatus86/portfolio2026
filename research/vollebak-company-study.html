Summary

Vollebak is a small London direct-to-consumer menswear brand that sells garments built around named, high-performance materials: graphene, Dyneema, carbon fiber, glass microspheres, silver-plated shielding fabric, chemical-suit rubber, metallic glass. Revenue is not public. It sells material stories and objects more than volume.

Buckle color: not found (metallic, no name given). - Black Squid Jacket: black with slight metallic sheen in dull light; color shifts across the visible spectrum in sun or flash. - Graphene Jacket and Carbon Fibre + Dyneema Jacket: colorways are not named on the pages I read; hand not stated beyond "ultra lightweight". Not found. The palette is narrow: black as the base, one loud signal color (yellow) when the material is the point, and surface effects (matte blast, iridescence, patina) doing the work that color does elsewhere. Useful as a reference for how a material-led line limits color and lets finish carry the story.

This is not proof of distress: strike-offs were discontinued, the company is "Active", and fresh equity was issued. I cannot say more without the 2025 accounts or a P&L (not public). The earlier reading that late filing "does not by itself show distress" still holds for one late filing; it no longer holds as the whole picture.

Materials worth a closer look (for soft materials, apparel, car interiors)

Hypotheses, not recommendations to buy or test: 1. Vegetable-tanned nappa with a metallized (silver-plated polyamide) lining: a soft-leather surface over a shielding layer; relevant to interior trim only if EMC shielding has a use case. Open: lining durability under flex. 2. Dyneema grid in nylon: abrasion and rip resistance at low weight; seat bolsters and door trim wear zones. 3. Polychloroprene-coated polyester (Fothergill): a rubberized, wipe-clean, high-chroma woven; relevant to wipe-clean trim and bright accents. Hand and flex life unknown. 4. Glass-microsphere resin coating (Black Squid): retroreflective, iridescent surface; durability and hand unknown. 5. Glass-microsphere blasted matte finish on laser-melted metallic glass: a non-directional matte for trim hardware. 6. Graphene-coated membrane or yarn: thermoregulation claims need independent data first. Limits: all from brand and press pages. No supplier-level or test data.
