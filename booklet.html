EEditorial · pp. 12–13 texture design process (technical evidence page)

Texture design process
Texture design process
Shingo Kawamura · 2026
The project investigates how a surface can carry weight, depth and calm. A fibre structure is drawn, tested as a render, and brought back to a physical sample. Which of its properties survive the step from image to cloth? Where does a digital texture stop and a material begin?
The resulting work is a range of surfaces, from soft to hard, set in repeat. Placeholder copy.
Each variation was judged on the same four things: scale, density, direction and the way the surface catches light. The strongest were taken forward to the fabric design stage.
