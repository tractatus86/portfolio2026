About this page

What this page is

Where design and market inputs are synthesized into images. A design input, such as a work, a material or an exhibition, is matched with a market input, such as a rule, a supply change or a category shift.

How to read it

Now is the piece in progress. Upcoming lists the pairs being developed, each tagged with the theme it comes from.

What each pair leaves open

Every pair ends with a question rather than a conclusion. That question is what the image is about.
