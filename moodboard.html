About this page

What this page is

A reference wall for the themes: art, craft, places and materials. Each image can be traced to its author, license and source.

What is allowed in

Only open-licensed images appear: public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA, each with its credit. Images with unclear rights, brand photography and licensed trend material stay off the page.

How the filters work

The top row filters by theme code and the row below by category. They combine: choose T10 and Textile to see only the textile references for that theme.
