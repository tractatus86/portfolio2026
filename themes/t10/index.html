About

One idea, from two sides

Part 1 follows one idea from two sides. T10 asks what a surface does to the person who touches it. WGSN's Restorers describe the buyer who wants less, better made, and calmer. The pages show the references, three material studies, and the materials I would specify.

Test

The test

Soft, but with presence: the tone shifts are quiet, the edge disappears, and the texture can be read up close.

Not slick: a gloss with nothing to read gives the hand nothing to hold.

Care shows in the material choice, the finish and the repair. It is the measure, not the softness.
