01Specify

Natural fibers: merino, linen

WGSN lists natural fibers, tactile fabric and artisanal finish among Restorers fashion cues (FC28, Fashion, p12). YETI uses Nuyarn merino spinning in waffle-knit and heavyweight styles (Textile World, 3 Nov 2026).

Textile World

Use: Ground fabric for garments and cabin textile. Matte, readable weave.

02Sample

Enzymatically recycled polyester

Samsara Eco uses enzymes to break textile and plastic waste into building blocks for new polyester. lululemon launched two run shorts with at least 50% enzymatically recycled polyester by weight, limited to Australia and New Zealand from 10 Nov 2026 (body fabric 86% recycled polyester, 14% elastane). A 10-year supply plan covers polyester and nylon 6,6.

lululemon newsroom

Company statements. Recycled content is by weight of the product, not of the fabric.

Use: Body fabric for the apparel set; knit backing under a natural face.

03Watch

Nylon-6 recovery (Nylene)

NylaNova supplies Nylene with caprolactam recovered from mixed nylon-6 waste; it claims over 90% recovery and 95% lower greenhouse gases against a third-party assessment (Sourcing Journal, Nov 2026).

Sourcing Journal

Use: Shell or lining fabric, if volume and the assessment hold up.

04Sample

Cactus leather (Desserto)

Made from Opuntia cactus leaves, sun-dried and processed, as a top layer over a foam layer and a textile base of polyester, cotton or a blend (review in PMC). The same review reports 1.3 to 2.0 kg CO2e per m2, partial biodegradability that depends on backing and coating, and some substances found in hazard screenings.

PMC review

Ask the supplier for the screening results and the exact backing before specifying.

Use: Cabin accents (bolster, pull, trim) and small goods. Test whether the grain reads in the hand, not only by eye.

05Watch

Bacterial and bio-based dye

Oxman's Vigils sprays engineered E. coli on 3D-knitted silk; after 24 hours indigo and melanin form in the fabric, one cape per batch, with no extensive life-cycle assessment and durability tests (wash, rub, light) still outstanding. Colorifix engineers microbes to make and fix pigment on fabric and has scaled to customer dyeing, per IKEA's profile.

Dezeen on Vigils

Vigils is a research prototype, not a production dye.

Use: Color route for muted indigo, amethyst and verdigris tones. Prototype for Vigils; ask Colorifix for fastness data.

06Watch

Spider silk (recombinant)

Kraig Biocraft Laboratories shipped its first commercial order of recombinant spider silk yarn to a sportswear brand for a pilot, announced 29 Sep 2026. The fiber blends spider silk and silkworm silk proteins; the company calls it biodegradable. Volumes and timing of further orders are not disclosed.

Business Insider / GlobeNewswire

Use: Accent yarn or hand-feel layer. A materials note only, until there is volume.

07Watch

Seaweed fiber (Uluu PHA with PLA)

Norrøna and Uluu are working toward an apparel fiber from seaweed-derived PHA blended with PLA, to be tested by a Norrøna supplier (Sourcing Journal, Nov 2026).

Sourcing Journal

Early development, no sales or volume reported.

Use: Research reference for the rematerialization argument (T3). No specification.

About this page

What this page is

A working list of materials I would name in a spec, sample before deciding, or keep watching.

What the tags mean

Specify: ready to name in a brief. Sample: promising, but it needs a physical swatch before a decision. Watch: not ready yet, tracked for change.

Where the facts come from

Each entry cites its source directly under the text, so a claim can be checked. The line marked Use is my own call on where the material could go.
