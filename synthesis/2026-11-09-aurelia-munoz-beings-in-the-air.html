Aurèlia Muñoz: tapestry that leaves the wall

"Beings in the air": macramé and tapestry as free-hanging structure. Muñoz's knotted forms drop the wall and become vertical, suspended bodies.

T1 Fabric as sculpture, sculpture as cloth · Material innovation

Why it matters

Lineage: nouvelle tapisserie and the Catalan tapestry school, Lausanne Biennials 1965 to 1983 (MACBA). Link to textiles/interiors: knot, tension and verticality are the same logic as headliner, pleat and suspended acoustic soft goods.

The idea

"She took tapestry off the wall and let it hang in the air."

MACBA's retrospective (opened 5 Nov 2026, runs to 2 May 2027) traces fifty years from hessian paintings to macramé "birds-kites" and handmade-paper mobiles. the interesting move is structural. Knotting makes the surface and the support the same thing, which is a useful lens for any soft material that must carry its own load. Pairing a study of knot geometry with her verticality shows how a textile can be architecture.
