Misha Kahn: a painting translated into upholstery wool (product / object design)

"From brushstroke to sofa." Kahn painted a composition, had textile studio JG SWITZER weave it in wool, then rebuilt the painted arrangement across upholstered forms.

T3 Dematerialization vs rematerialization · Material innovation

Why it matters

In "All Intensive Purposes" at Friedman Benda, New York (opened 29 Oct 2026, to 12 Dec 2026 per designboom radar), the new upholstered pieces began as a painted composition translated into wool by JG SWITZER; Kahn says conventional upholstery limits control over the surface (designboom, 3 Nov 2026). A clean example of the painting-to-weave-to-form pipeline, directly relevant to custom surface design for seating.

The idea

Paint it first, then make the fabric match the painting.

Kahn reverses the usual order: the artwork comes first and the textile is built to serve it. That is the part worth borrowing, because most upholstery starts from an existing cloth. A weave has its own resolution, so check what your painting loses and gains once it becomes yarn.
