Tapa: cloth as a gift (fine art / lineage)

"Cloth that carries rank" – a pattern study of Polynesian bark cloth grids and stripes as early surface design.

T8 Asia and postcolonial frame · Textile structure

Why it matters

Lineage: tapa (masi, siapo, hiapo) is bark cloth with geometric designs painted, stamped or stenciled, from a textile tradition the Met dates to over 3,500 years in Southeast Asian islands. Link to interiors/CMF: repeat, border and stripe logic as a system for wall, upholstery and trim.

The idea

A 19th-century bark cloth already solved the stripe, border and repeat problem.

The Met opened a small focused show of 13 works from Fiji, Samoa, Futuna and Niue on 2 Nov 2026. What interests me as a CMF designer is how the cloth is also an instrument of exchange: pattern signals status. I redrew one panel to see how border, medallion and stripe carry hierarchy, then asked what that hierarchy would look like as a woven seat fabric.
