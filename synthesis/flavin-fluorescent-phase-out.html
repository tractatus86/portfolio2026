Flavin and the fluorescent phase-out

A design input paired with a market input, shown as four colorway mocks for a carousel. The question it leaves: what do we match when a lamp, a dye or a yarn is discontinued?

(01)(02)(03)(04)

The pair

  • Design input: Dan Flavin, "Some Light" opens at Guggenheim Bilbao on 11 Nov 2026. His medium is the fluorescent tube. Images in the design note.
  • Market input: the Minamata Convention ends manufacture, import and export of linear fluorescent lamps for general lighting (2027 triband, 2026 halophosphate over 40 W). Existing stock may still be used.
