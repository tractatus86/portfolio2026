Colored strips as a corridor: Alvaro Barrington, "Western Beef ft. Western Union" (fine art)

"A curtain you walk through." Floor-to-ceiling colored PVC strips fill the open apertures of an unfinished museum in Accra, with colors drawn from kente weavers.

T8 Asia and postcolonial frame · Trend colours

Why it matters

Barrington's first presentation on the African continent, at Limbo Museum, University of Ghana campus, Accra, runs 31 Oct 2026 to 1 Feb 2027. The strips recall supermarket freezer curtains, and their colors come from his observation of Accra and of kente weavers during a residency (designboom, 18 Nov 2026). He works across painting, textiles, sculpture and installation. A soft, hanging, colored surface read through light and movement is a textile-and-interiors problem: translucency, overlap, and how a hue changes as you pass through it. Lineage: kente is built from narrow woven bands joined side by side; the strips echo that band logic in an industrial material.

The idea

What if a color study were something you could walk through?

Barrington takes a cheap, everyday partition and lets color and light do the work of a painting, with weaving as the quiet reference. For a color-and-materials designer, it is a lesson in how band width, translucency and overlap change a hue. Pair a kente band structure with a strip-curtain structure and compare what each does to light.
