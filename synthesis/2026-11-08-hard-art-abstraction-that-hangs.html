Hard Art: abstraction that hangs, drapes and stretches (fine art)

"Abstraction you can feel" - MoMA PS1's Hard Art, read through soft and flexible materials.

T1 Fabric as sculpture, sculpture as cloth · Textile structure

Why it matters

The show (opened 5 Nov 2026) covers abstraction by Black artists in the US from 1969 to today, 40+ artists, 90+ works. Link to textiles: surface, tension and drape as design variables.

The idea

What happens to a painting when you let go of the stretcher?

MoMA PS1 opened Hard Art this week, tracing abstraction by Black artists from 1969 on. For anyone who designs with cloth, the period's habit of letting material hang, sag and stretch is a free lesson in drape and tension. The pairing shows fabric behaving as the structure itself, not a surface applied to one.
