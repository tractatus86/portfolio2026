Sabine Marcelis: light and colour as material (product / object design)

"Color is a material property" - Marcelis's first museum show, Light and Color, in Zurich.

T3 Dematerialization vs rematerialization · Trend colours

Why it matters

Exhibition runs 30 Oct 2026 to 4 Apr 2027 at Museum für Gestaltung Zürich (Toni-Areal). Per designboom: glass, resin, stone and mirror samples and studies shown next to finished works; eight 2.56 m Mathmos lava columns in pastel pink in a mirrored room. Link to textiles: translucency, sheen and CMF sample thinking apply directly to coated and backlit soft materials in interiors.

The idea

The sample board is the exhibition.

Marcelis shows material studies beside the finished pieces, so you read color as a result of thickness, transparency and reflection, not a paint choice. That is CMF in a museum. The post pairs one swatch stack with its own reflection to show the same idea at desk scale.
