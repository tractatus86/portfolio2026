Fried, not fired: Cecil Nabenashi, "Tempura Object" (product / craft)

"What if frying were a forming method like firing or casting?"

T9 Wear, fragility and the trace of time · Material innovation

Why it matters

At Designart Tokyo 2026 (30 Oct to 8 Nov), Tokyo Midtown Galleria, Cecil Nabenashi shows vases and bowls made from paper and fibers coated in batter and deep-fried; the exhibition text compares it to firing ceramics or casting, a way of solidifying material between chance and control (designweek.app listing for Designart Tokyo 2026). Dezeen (5 Nov 2026) reports she used a batter of specially developed non-edible ingredients and that her next challenge is a deep-fried garment. She was selected for the festival's UNDER 30 program (PR Times release, Designart Tokyo). Lineage: craft as a set of techniques for controlling material (the show's own framing). For a soft-materials designer this is a finishing study: a coating plus heat changes color, hardness and surface of a fiber structure, which is close to how stiffeners, resins and heat-set finishes work on textiles. Durability and safety are untested here.

The idea

She fried paper to make a bowl, and the oil did the sculpting.

Frying here is a hardening step, like a finish, not a recipe. Pair the bowls with your own before-and-after yarn test.
