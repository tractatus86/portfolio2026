Nomura, "Pulling Apart": an adhesive that lets go with one pull

A strong, flexible tape that holds heavy panels, then stretches and peels off cleanly when pulled, so parts can be recycled.

T9 Wear, fragility and the trace of time · Sustainability

Why it matters

Tokyo studio Nomura shows the tape in "Separation: Designed to Come Apart", a Daikanyama exhibition of products made for disassembly, during Designart Tokyo (30 Oct to 8 Nov 2026). Nomura says glue on timber or metal often leaves marks or blocks recycling; here one pull undoes the bond. It uses easy-disassembly bonding technology from Aica Kogyo and is still a prototype (Dezeen, 5 Nov 2026). Soft trim, headliners and upholstery are usually glued to panels; a peel-away bond is a design question about repair and recycling at end of life.

The idea

What if the way a thing comes apart were the first design decision?

Most trim is designed for how it goes on. Nomura designs the exit: one tab, one pull. Put a fabric-covered panel next to it and ask where a pull tab could sit without showing.
