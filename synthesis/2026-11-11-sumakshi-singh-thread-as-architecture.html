Thread as architecture: Sumakshi Singh, "Permanent Address" (fine art / fiber art)

"A house made of thread." A demolished family home rebuilt life-size in translucent white thread, shown next to handwoven cotton by Ranjani Shettar.

T9 Wear, fragility and the trace of time · Textile structure

Why it matters

At the India Pavilion, 61st Venice Biennale, Singh rebuilds fragments of her demolished New Delhi family home in white thread, drawing on embroidery traditions passed through generations of women; Shettar's "Under the same sky" combines handwoven cotton, steel and lacquer (designboom, 16 Jun 2026). Lineage: embroidery and handwork as an archive of domestic space. For an interiors and soft-materials designer, this is cloth standing in for walls: sheerness, density and light are the structure.

The idea

What if a wall were only thread, and the light did the rest?

Singh remakes a lost house from thread alone, so what you see is the idea of the building, not the building. As someone who works with density and openness in cloth, I read it as a construction study: how few yarns can still hold a room? Pair it with a sheer of your own and compare how each one lets light through.
