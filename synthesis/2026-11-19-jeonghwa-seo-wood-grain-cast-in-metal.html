Wood grain cast in metal: Jeonghwa Seo, "Precise Play" at Carwan Gallery Hong Kong (product / object design)

"The grain stays, the wood goes." Seo's sand-cast aluminum keeps the imprint of timber, shown beside stools that pair twelve contrasting materials.

T9 Wear, fragility and the trace of time · Material innovation

Why it matters

"Precise Play" opens 20 Nov 2026 at Carwan Gallery's new Hong Kong space and runs to 20 Feb 2027. Seo, born in Seoul in 1982, transfers wood grain onto aluminum by sand casting and shows furniture, lighting and objects in cast aluminum, wood, resin and rush. His Material Container series pairs twelve materials, from basalt and maple to brass and acrylic (Carwan Gallery and GalleriesNow pages, 2026). This is a surface-texture study across materials, the question a CMF designer asks when a trim, a knit and a leather must read as one family: how touch, temperature and density change what a surface says.

The idea

Which is the material, and which is the memory of it?

Seo lets metal remember wood. For a designer who has to make fabric, plastic and trim agree in one cabin, it is a reminder that a surface can carry the history of another material. Try the pairing on your own board: one real texture, one cast of it.
